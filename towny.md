# Towny

Towny lets players establish towns, claim land, build communities, and manage local rules. Exact commands, costs, limits, and protections depend on the server configuration.

## Joining a town

Joining an established town is a good way to find land, meet other players, and participate in a community.

- Ask the mayor or town staff about plot prices and town rules.
- Check whether the town has taxes or other recurring costs.
- Confirm what happens to your plot and belongings if you leave.
- Follow the town's published rules as well as the server rules.

## Creating a town

Before founding a town, make sure you understand the costs and ongoing obligations.

1. Find a suitable location.
2. Check nearby claims and world restrictions.
3. Confirm the town creation price and upkeep.
4. Use the configured Towny command to create the town.
5. Set town permissions and invite residents.
6. Establish clear rules for plots, taxes, and shared areas.

## Town leadership

Mayors and assistants should communicate taxes, plot rules, and major changes clearly. Leadership permissions should be given carefully and removed when no longer needed.

Recommended town practices:

- Publish taxes and plot costs.
- Explain town kick or eviction policies.
- Keep public services and entrances accessible.
- Use trusted assistants and review their permissions.
- Document agreements involving expensive shared builds.
