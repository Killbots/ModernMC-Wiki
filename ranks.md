# Ranks & Progression

Ranks recognize progression and may provide convenience perks, cosmetics, or access to additional features. The goal is to reward commitment without making the server unfair to players who do not purchase perks.

## Rank types

BetterTowny may use separate systems for **staff roles**, **gameplay progression**, and **cosmetic tags**. These systems serve different purposes and should not be confused with one another.

## What ranks can include

- Extra home slots, within a clearly stated limit.
- Access to convenience commands, where balanced.
- Cosmetic prefixes, name colours, particles, or titles.
- Non-exclusive quality-of-life features.
- Recognition for playtime, achievements, community contributions, or events.

## Fairness principles

1. Publish every perk and its limits before a player obtains a rank.
2. Avoid perks that directly guarantee wins in PvP or competitive events.
3. Do not imply that a cosmetic purchase is required to enjoy core gameplay.
4. Clearly distinguish earned ranks from paid supporter perks.
5. State whether perks stack and what happens when a rank expires.
