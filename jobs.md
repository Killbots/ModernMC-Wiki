# Jobs

Jobs let players earn money by participating in supported activities. Choose a job that fits your normal playstyle, then review its income rates and limits before relying on it as your main source of money.

## Choosing a job

| Playstyle | Job examples | Typical activities |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering | Miner, Woodcutter, Digger | Collecting supported resources |
| Farming | Farmer, Herbalist | Growing and harvesting crops |
| Combat | Hunter, Warrior | Defeating eligible mobs or participating in supported combat |
| Crafting | Crafter, Smelter | Making or processing supported items |
| Fishing | Fisher | Catching eligible fish |
| Building | Builder | Placing supported blocks, if enabled |

These are examples, not a guarantee that every job is installed. Replace them with the exact job names and actions in your configuration.

## Job progression and balance

The aim is to make several playstyles viable rather than allowing one activity to dominate the economy. Rates may be adjusted if a job becomes substantially more profitable than others.

## Abuse prevention

Do not use automated farms, alternate accounts, repeated placement/breaking loops, or other methods to generate unintended income. The exact limits are set by the server rules and job configuration.

**Why did I not get paid?**  
The action may not be eligible, the world may be restricted, or you may have reached a limit. Check the job information and ask staff if it appears to be a bug.

**Can jobs be combined with mcMMO?**  
They may progress at the same time if both plugins are enabled and the relevant actions are allowed. Neither system should be assumed to reward every action.
