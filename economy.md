# Economy

The BetterTowny economy is designed to support trading, town development, player shops, and long-term goals. Prices and income sources may be adjusted as the server economy develops.

## Ways to earn money

- **Jobs:** earn wages by completing supported activities.
- **Player trading:** sell goods or services to other players.
- **Player shops:** sell items to other players, if enabled.
- **Quests and events:** receive published rewards for completing objectives.
- **Town services:** offer building, gathering, transport, or other services to players.

## Spending money

Common expenses may include:

- Town creation and upkeep.
- Town and plot claims.
- Shop setup or market rent.
- Materials, tools, and other player-to-player purchases.
- Quest or event services where a cost is clearly disclosed.

See the **[Towny](towny.md)** page for land and town systems.

## Player trading

Before confirming a trade:

1. Agree on the item, quantity, and price.
2. Check item details and enchantments.
3. Use the server's approved trade or shop systems when available.
4. Keep evidence if a trade is disputed.
5. Report scams through **[EDIT: support process]**.

Staff decisions on player trades follow **[EDIT: server policy]**. Do not promise refunds unless the published policy provides for them.

## Economy rules

- Do not exploit duplication bugs, shop bugs, or unintended money-generation loops.
- Do not use alternate accounts to bypass published limits.
- Do not impersonate staff or claim that a transaction is staff-approved when it is not.
- Report major economy exploits instead of spreading or abusing them.
